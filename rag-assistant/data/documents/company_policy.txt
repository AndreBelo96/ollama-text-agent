ACME Tech - Company Policy

WORKING HOURS
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
Flexible working hours are allowed with manager approval. Core hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
Remote work is allowed up to 3 days per week. Full remote must be approved by HR.

VACATION AND TIME OFF
Employees receive 25 vacation days per year.
Vacation days must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance via the HR portal.
Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year.
Sick days are separate from vacation days. Employees have 10 sick days per year.
National holidays are paid days off and do not count against vacation days.

BENEFITS
Health insurance is fully covered by the company for the employee.
Family members can be added to the health plan at a discounted rate of 30%.
Employees receive a monthly meal voucher of 150 euros.
A budget of 1000 euros per year is available for training and conferences.
gym membership is reimbursed up to 50 euros per month.

EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
Work-related expenses must be submitted within 30 days with receipts.
Travel expenses are reimbursed at standard rates approved by finance.
Expenses over 500 euros require manager approval before being incurred.
