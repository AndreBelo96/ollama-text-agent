ACME Tech - Hiring Process

OVERVIEW
ACME Tech follows a structured hiring process to ensure quality and fairness.
All open positions are posted on the internal job board and external platforms.
Referrals from current employees are encouraged and rewarded with a 1000 euro bonus if the candidate is hired.

STAGES
Stage 1 - Application Review: HR screens resumes within 5 business days.
Stage 2 - HR Call: 30 minute call to assess culture fit and salary expectations.
Stage 3 - Technical Interview: 60-90 minute interview with the hiring team.
Stage 4 - Technical Assignment: A take-home assignment relevant to the role. Maximum 4 hours of work expected.
Stage 5 - Final Interview: 45 minute interview with the team lead or CTO for senior roles.
Stage 6 - Offer: HR sends the offer within 3 business days of the final interview.

TIMELINE
The full process typically takes 3 to 5 weeks from application to offer.
Candidates are updated at every stage within 5 business days.
Ghosting candidates is strictly not allowed.

COMPENSATION
Junior roles: 28,000 to 38,000 euros per year.
Mid roles: 38,000 to 55,000 euros per year.
Senior roles: 55,000 to 80,000 euros per year.
Lead and Principal roles: 80,000 to 110,000 euros per year.
All offers include stock options and the standard benefits package.

DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION
ACME Tech is committed to building diverse teams.
All interviewers must complete unconscious bias training before conducting interviews.
Job descriptions are reviewed for inclusive language before posting.
