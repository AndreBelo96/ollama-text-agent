ACME Tech - Incident Management

SEVERITY LEVELS
SEV1 - Critical: complete service outage or data loss. Immediate response required 24/7.
SEV2 - High: major feature broken, significant user impact. Response within 1 hour.
SEV3 - Medium: partial degradation, workaround available. Response within 4 hours.
SEV4 - Low: minor issue, minimal user impact. Response within 1 business day.

RESPONSE PROCESS
1. Detection: incident detected via monitoring alerts or user report.
2. Declaration: on-call engineer declares incident and opens a war room on Slack (#incidents).
3. Assignment: incident commander assigned. For SEV1 a senior engineer is paged immediately.
4. Communication: status page updated within 15 minutes of SEV1/SEV2 declaration.
5. Resolution: fix deployed and verified.
6. Post-mortem: written within 5 business days for SEV1 and SEV2 incidents.

ON-CALL
On-call rotation is weekly and covers all engineers with more than 6 months tenure.
On-call engineers receive a 200 euro monthly stipend.
SEV1 pages outside business hours receive a 100 euro bonus per incident.

POST-MORTEM
Post-mortems are blameless — the goal is to improve systems, not assign fault.
They must include: timeline, root cause, impact, action items with owners and deadlines.
Action items must be completed within 30 days.

TOOLS
Monitoring: Grafana and Prometheus.
Alerting: PagerDuty.
Communication: Slack #incidents channel.
Status page: status.acmetech.com.
