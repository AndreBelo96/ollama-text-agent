ACME Tech - Onboarding Guide

WEEK 1
Day 1: HR orientation, sign contracts, receive equipment.
Day 2: IT setup — laptop configuration, access to GitHub, Slack, and internal tools.
Day 3-4: Meet your team, attend daily standups, read technical documentation.
Day 5: First task assignment — usually a small bug fix or documentation update.

WEEK 2-4
Shadow senior developers on ongoing projects.
Complete mandatory security training available on the internal LMS.
Set up local development environment using the onboarding repository.
Attend your first sprint planning and retrospective.

FIRST 3 MONTHS
Complete at least one full feature from design to deployment.
Schedule monthly 1:1 with your manager to discuss progress and goals.
Join at least one internal community of practice (AI, DevOps, Frontend, etc.)

TOOLS ACCESS
Request access to tools via the IT portal at it.acmetech.internal.
Standard tools provisioned on day 1: Slack, GitHub, Jira, Confluence.
Access to production systems is granted after 30 days and security training completion.
