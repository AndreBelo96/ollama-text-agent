ACME Tech - Remote Work Policy

ELIGIBILITY
All full-time employees are eligible for remote work after completing the onboarding period.
Contractors and part-time employees may work remotely with manager approval.
Remote work is not available during the first 30 days of employment.

HYBRID POLICY
Employees are expected in the office at least 2 days per week.
Team days are Tuesday and Thursday — attendance is strongly encouraged.
Full remote work (0 days in office) requires written approval from HR and the team lead.
Fully remote employees outside the city receive a 100 euro monthly home office stipend.

HOME OFFICE REQUIREMENTS
Employees are responsible for maintaining a productive home work environment.
A reliable internet connection of at least 50 Mbps is required.
ACME Tech provides a one-time 500 euro home office equipment budget.
All work must be done on company-issued or approved devices.

AVAILABILITY
Remote employees must be reachable during core hours: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
Camera on is expected during team meetings unless there is a valid reason.
Response time to messages during work hours should not exceed 30 minutes.

INTERNATIONAL REMOTE WORK
Working remotely from abroad is allowed for up to 30 days per year.
Tax and legal implications are the employee's responsibility.
Notify HR at least 2 weeks in advance for international remote work.
Some countries may be restricted due to legal or security reasons — check with HR first.
